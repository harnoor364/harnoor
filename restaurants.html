The big question

Do I need a website if I'm already on Zomato and Swiggy?

Short answer: yes, but not to replace Zomato and Swiggy. Use the apps to get discovered, and use your own website to keep the customers you've already won.

Why the apps alone aren't enough

  • You pay a commission on every order, including orders from your regular customers who would have ordered from you anyway.
  • You don't own the customer. The app keeps their number and order history, and next time it can show them your competitor first.
  • Your page looks like everyone else's. On the app you're one listing among hundreds; your own website shows your story, your best photos and your full menu.
  • Many orders never go through apps: parties, catering, table bookings and bulk orders come directly to restaurants that are easy to contact.

What your own website does that the apps can't

  1. Direct WhatsApp orders from regular customers, with no commission on those orders.
  2. Shows up when people Google your name, so they don't land on a third-party page.
  3. Brings in party and catering enquiries, often your most valuable orders.
  4. Lets you control your menu, prices and offers any time.

The smart way: use both

Keep Zomato and Swiggy for new customers. Then turn them into direct customers: put a QR code to your website menu on your bills, packaging and tables, and share your WhatsApp ordering link. Over time, more of your repeat orders come to you directly.
