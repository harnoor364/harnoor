Why does a hosiery business in Ludhiana need its own website?

So buyers find you, trust you and contact you directly. Ludhiana is known across India for hosiery and knitwear, which means a buyer searching online has many manufacturers to choose from. A clear website for your hosiery business helps you stand out.

Why WhatsApp catalogues and marketplaces aren't enough

  • Marketplace listings put you next to your competitors, and buyers compare you mainly on price.
  • WhatsApp catalogues only reach people who already have your number. New buyers searching on Google never see them.
  • A website builds trust with buyers in other cities who can't visit your factory before their first order.

What a website for a hosiery business should include

  1. Your range by category, with real photos of your products.
  2. Who you supply: retailers, distributors, exporters or private-label orders.
  3. Order details: minimum order quantity, sizes and dispatch information.
  4. WhatsApp and call buttons on every page, plus an enquiry form.
  5. Your address on Google Maps and a complete Google Business Profile.
