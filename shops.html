Will a website really bring more customers to my shop?

Yes, if people can find it. Customers don't just walk past shops any more; they search first. A website, together with your Google Business Profile, makes sure your shop shows up in those searches.

How customers find shops today

Think about searches like “saree shop near me”, “mobile repair in Ludhiana” or “hardware store open now”. Google shows the shops it trusts most: ones with a complete Google Business Profile, good reviews and a website that clearly says what they sell, where they are and when they're open.

What a shop website actually does for you

  1. Gets you found in “near me” searches and on Google Maps.
  2. Answers questions before they call: what you sell, brands, price range, timings and parking.
  3. Builds trust: a professional website makes a new customer far more comfortable visiting or ordering.
  4. Brings WhatsApp enquiries and orders, even when the customer can't visit.
  5. Promotes your offers during festivals and wedding season, when people are actively looking.

What a website won't do on its own

A website is not magic: it works best when your Google Business Profile is complete, you collect reviews from happy customers, and you share your website link on WhatsApp, bills and your shop board. I help with all of these.

Frequently asked questions

I already sell on WhatsApp and Instagram. Why do I need a website?

WhatsApp and Instagram are great for existing customers, but they rarely appear when new customers search on Google. A website does, and it makes your shop look established and trustworthy.

Do I need to put all my products and prices online?

No. Many shops show their main categories, brands and price ranges, and let customers ask on WhatsApp for exact prices and stock.

Can I sell online later?

Yes. You can start with a simple website and add a product catalogue with online payments later (Premium package).
