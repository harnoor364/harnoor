How to choose a website designer in Ludhiana

Search for a website designer in Ludhiana and you'll find hundreds of options, from students and freelancers to large agencies. Here's how to pick the right one for a small business, without paying for things you don't need.

Freelancer or website design company?

Many business owners search for a website design company in Ludhiana because "company" sounds safer. But for a clinic, shop or restaurant, the size of the team matters less than who actually works on your website. With a big agency, you usually talk to a salesperson while a junior designer builds the site. With an independent designer, the person you speak to is the person who designs it, answers your WhatsApp and fixes things later.

A company makes sense for large projects like big online stores, apps or ongoing marketing. For a professional business website, a skilled independent designer is usually faster, more personal and more affordable. That's how I work as a freelance website designer in Ludhiana: you deal with me from your first WhatsApp message to launch day, and after it.

5 questions to ask before you hire anyone

  1. Can I see websites you've built? Open them on your phone. Most of your customers will.
  2. What exactly is included in the price? Number of pages, contact forms, WhatsApp button, Google Maps, SEO setup.
  3. Who owns the website and domain? They should be registered in your name.
  4. What happens after launch? Ask how changes and support work, and what they cost.
  5. How will customers find the site? A good designer sets up the basics: page titles, a sitemap, Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile.

What a good small-business website needs

  • Loads fast and looks good on mobile phones
  • Shows clearly what you do, where you are and your timings
  • One-tap WhatsApp and call buttons
  • Google Maps directions and an enquiry or booking form
  • Real photos, real reviews and clear prices where possible
