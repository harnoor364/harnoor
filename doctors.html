Guide

What every doctor's website in Ludhiana should have

Patients judge a clinic before they ever walk in. Good website design for doctors isn't about flashy effects. It's about making the right information easy to find and building trust.

1. The basics, visible straight away

Your name, speciality, clinic address, OPD timings and phone number should be on the first screen. These are the first things patients look for, and they shouldn't have to scroll to find them.

2. A simple way to request an appointment

A short appointment request form (name, phone, preferred day) plus WhatsApp and call buttons. Keep it short: ask only for what you need to call the patient back, and don't collect medical details through a website form.

3. Google Maps and your Google Business Profile

When someone searches "child specialist near me" or "dentist near me", Google shows the map listings first. Your website and your Google Business Profile should have exactly the same clinic name, address and phone number, and your site should link to directions.

4. Your qualifications and services, explained simply

List your degrees, experience and the conditions or treatments you handle, in plain language that patients understand. A clear page for each main service also helps you appear in Google searches for those services.

5. Professional and responsible content

Medical websites should stay factual. Avoid claims like "best doctor in the city" or guaranteed results, and keep your website in line with the National Medical Commission's guidelines on how doctors may publicise their practice. A calm, honest website builds more trust than an advert.

6. Fast, mobile-first and secure

Most patients will visit on a phone, sometimes on slow data. The site should load quickly, have large buttons and use HTTPS (the padlock in the browser).
