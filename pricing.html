Guide

How much does a website cost in Ludhiana?

Ask around and you'll hear prices from ₹3,000 to several lakhs. Here's what actually changes the price, and how to get affordable website design in Ludhiana without ending up with a website that doesn't bring customers.

What changes the website design cost in Ludhiana

  • Number of pages: a 5-page site (Home, About, Services, Gallery, Contact) costs less than a 10-page site with a page for each service.
  • Features: booking forms, online payments, menus, product catalogues and blogs take more time to build.
  • Custom design vs. ready layout: a design made only for your brand costs more than adapting an existing layout.
  • Setup for Google: SEO basics, Google Maps and Google Business Profile setup help customers find you.

Watch out for "cheap" websites

A very low price often means the site is slow on mobile, isn't set up for Google, or the domain is registered in the designer's name instead of yours, so you can't move or change the site without them. A website is only cheap if it brings you customers.

Other costs to plan for

Besides design, every website needs a domain name (your .com or .in address) and hosting, both renewed every year. Simple websites like the ones I build can usually be hosted at low cost. I'll explain the options before you decide.
