Is a Google Business Profile enough, or do I need a website too?

You need both, and they work best together. Your Google Business Profile gets you onto the map; your website convinces people to choose you.

What a Google Business Profile does well

Your free Google Business Profile shows your business on Google Maps and in the local results when people search “salon near me” or “AC repair in Ludhiana”. It shows your reviews, photos, timings and a call button. Every service business should have one, fully completed.

Where a profile alone falls short

  • Limited space: you can't properly explain your services, packages, prices or qualifications.
  • You look like everyone else: profiles all look the same, so customers compare only stars and distance.
  • Fewer searches: people searching for a specific service (“bridal makeup Ludhiana”, “JEE physics tuition”) often click on websites, not just the map.
  • Google rewards it: profiles that link to a good website with matching details usually rank better locally.

How they work together

  1. A customer finds you on Google Maps through your profile.
  2. They tap your website link to see your services, prices, work and reviews.
  3. They book, call or message you on WhatsApp straight from the website.
